Why the Orators Camp

Speaking up is a skill. Skills can be practised.

Around 2 in 10 people would do almost anything to avoid speaking in public. We start young, so speaking up feels normal long before it matters for exams, interviews and leadership.

Get to know KL Debate

Public speaking

Voice, posture and structure. Students plan short speeches, deliver them on the clock and get specific feedback.

Critical thinking

Building arguments, weighing evidence and spotting weak reasoning, including their own.

Debate

Taking a side, rebutting with respect and disagreeing well, in real practice debates with a motion and a timer.

Give them a head start Ages 7 to 18 · no experience needed
How we teach

Hands-on from the first minute.

No long lectures. Every session is built around students standing up, speaking and trying again, in a friendly, non-competitive room.

  1. 01

    Simulations

    Practice in real speaking situations, not just theory.

  2. 02

    Timed prep, timed speeches

    Short, clocked rounds so students learn to think and speak under a little pressure.

  3. 03

    Live polls

    The room votes on who persuaded them, so students see what actually works.

  4. 04

    Honest reflection

    Every session ends with what went well and one thing to try next time.
